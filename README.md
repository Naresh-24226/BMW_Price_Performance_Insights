# 📊 BMW Price & Performance Insights - Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard designed in Power BI to analyze the BMW used car market, evaluating pricing trajectories, inventory distribution, and performance metrics across 11,000 vehicles.

---

## 🚀 Executive Summary
This report provides a data-driven overview of the BMW used car market based on key performance indicators (KPIs) including inventory volume, historical price trends, mileage distribution, and fuel efficiency metrics. 

### 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Inventory Volume:** 11,000 Units — Robust sample size representing diversified models.
* **Average Market Price (Avg Price):** 22.69K — Mean market valuation across the fleet spectrum.
* **Average Fleet Mileage (Avg Mileage):** 25.71K Miles/KM — Demonstrates a relatively low-mileage asset composition.
* **Fuel Efficiency Index (Avg MPG):** 56.48 MPG — Reflects a highly fuel-efficient inventory blend.

---

## 🔍 Visualizations & Deep-Dive Analysis

### 1. Price Trend Over Time (Line Chart)
* **Observation:** The market witnessed an exponential rise post-2012, peaking close to the 40K mark towards 2020.
* **Insight:** The sharp increase points to either a shift towards premium high-spec models or strong brand equity retention for newer BMW generations.

### 2. Volume & Price Analytics by Model (Combo Chart)
* **Volume Leaders:** The **3 Series** and **1 Series** emerge as the massive volume drivers, commanding the largest share of inventory.
* **Premium Value Drivers:** While the **M4** model represents a tiny fraction of the volume, it commands the absolute highest peak in average price.

### 3. Fuel Type Breakdown (Donut Chart)
* **Distribution:** Diesel (**65.53%**), Petrol (**31.32%**), Hybrid & Electric (**2.79%**).
* **Insight:** The portfolio is heavily weighted toward Diesel variants, explaining the high overall fleet efficiency average.
